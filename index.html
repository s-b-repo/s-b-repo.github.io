// case study

Proven results in the real world.

National Government · 9-Day Engagement 01

Full-scope penetration test — external, internal, wireless, physical

Full-scope penetration test of a national government department. Found 36 vulnerabilities — 18 critical, including SharePoint 2007 still running in production (EOL 2017, 18 years old). Retrieved citizen SA ID numbers and personal records from an unsecured server — a POPIA breach requiring mandatory disclosure to the Information Regulator.

Achieved domain admin via EternalBlue against an unpatched Windows 7 server, extracted 5 staff credentials. Bypassed weapons screening at reception, tailgated through the secure entrance, and reached a private office undetected. The Wi-Fi ran WEP encryption with an open guest network. DMARC was monitoring-only — departmental emails fully spoofable. Not one alert was raised.

  • 36 vulnerabilities — 18 critical, 9 high
  • 5 confirmed breaches exploited on-site
  • Citizen PII extracted — POPIA triggered
  • Physical + wireless + internal + external
National ISP / MSP · Gap Assessment 02

57 hosts. 50+ exposed VM images. 7 downstream orgs affected.

White-box penetration test across 3 subnets of a national ISP and managed service provider. Extracted 27 employee password hashes from 10 unique accounts across 8 mounted VMs — all sharing an identical SHA-512 hash. Pulled the full FortiGate FG200F configuration including 17 encrypted credentials, an upstream water utility's AD LDAP bind, 9 VPN users, 3 RADIUS secrets, and 5 SSL private keys.

Discovered 50+ QCOW2 and 6 VMDK disk images on unauthenticated NFS shares — full Veeam backup repositories including a 2.6 TB Exchange email database. Mapped 4 Active Directory domains with 21 Windows hosts — all enumerated without credentials. The exposure chain cascaded to 7 downstream organizations across government, transport, manufacturing, and export credit insurance.

  • 37 vulns — 12 critical, 11 high
  • 27 hashes extracted from 10 accounts
  • 4 AD domains mapped, 21 hosts, zero creds
  • 7 downstream organizations secured
