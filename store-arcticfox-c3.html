// what it is

A C3 that hides in services you already trust

Arcticfox C3 is an asynchronous networking and codec framework in Rust that carries command-and-control content over services you already trust — GitHub, GitLab and paste sites — instead of a dedicated, attacker-owned server. It exists to study a whole class of covert channels, and to test whether your detection actually sees them.

The framework builds to an arcticfox-mcp binary, so it plugs straight into an AI-driven engagement — it is one of the C3 backends bundled with OpenHack — as well as running standalone for red-team and detection-validation work.

Treat it as a detection-validation instrument: stand it up in a lab or an authorized engagement, run traffic through the third-party channels, and confirm your monitoring flags the pattern — or find out that it doesn’t.
