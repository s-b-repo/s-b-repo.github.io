// what it is

The physical-access half of a red-team

Cardsz is a comprehensive, practical methodology for assessing RFID and NFC access-control systems with a Proxmark3 — covering Mifare Classic, HID Prox, EM4100, iCLASS, Mifare Ultralight, NTAG and DESFire.

It walks the real attack paths for each card family, so it reads as the field guide behind the badge-cloning portion of a physical engagement — what to try, in what order, and what each result means.

// what's inside

What it covers

A methodology, not a program — the how-to behind a physical assessment.

Per-family attack paths

Mifare Classic, HID Prox, EM4100, iCLASS, Ultralight, NTAG and DESFire, each with its own approach.

Proxmark3 workflow

The commands and sequence for reading, analysing and testing cards with a Proxmark3.

Cloning & emulation

How credentials are read, cloned and emulated — and how to tell when it worked.

Defence guidance

What the results mean for the access-control system, and how to make it harder to clone.
