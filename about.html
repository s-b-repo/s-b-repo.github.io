// about

We are Cyber Sec.

Cyber Sec is an offensive security practice built on published work — over 300 public repositories, precision-built tooling, and one outcome: proving where you are exposed, then helping you close it. We don't run scanners and call it a day. We build custom tooling, write exploit chains, tear apart code line-by-line, and research the vulnerabilities others miss. We are the firm you call when security cannot be a checkbox.

Engineered from the ground up.

Founded by Stephan Botes — a self-taught security engineer with 280+ public repositories spanning Rust, Go, Python, and C — Cyber Sec was built on a simple conviction: security is not a product you buy, it's a discipline you practice. No legacy overhead. No enterprise bloat. No sales-first culture. Just deep technical expertise, custom tooling, and evidence-based results.

We protect the organizations that society depends on — finance, healthcare, energy, defense, critical infrastructure — without the noise, the upsells, or the checkbox mentality that defines the legacy security industry.

We don't do security theater.

We audit what you actually run — not what your vendor's whitepaper claims. We test the real attack surface — not the sanitized demo environment. And we will tell you when your stack is the problem, because the most dangerous vulnerability in any organization is the one nobody wants to name out loud: the platforms you trust by default.

Default trust is how attackers win. Every APT, every ransomware operator, every supply-chain compromise — they rarely break in through some exotic zero-day. They walk through the front door you left unlocked because a vendor promised the lock was fine. The attack surface isn't just your code. It's every platform you chose to trust without verifying.

// methodology

How we execute.

Clean, transparent, repeatable — from scope to sign-off.

  1. 01

    Scoping & Authorization

    Documented scope, rules of engagement, written authorization. Every time. No exceptions.

  2. 02

    Assessment

    Manual testing by senior specialists, supported by custom-built tooling. No scanner-only assessments — ever.

  3. 03

    Reporting

    Findings ranked by business impact with reproducible evidence, concrete remediation, and an executive summary leadership can act on.

  4. 04

    Verification

    We re-test after your team implements fixes. You don't pay for a report — you pay for confirmation that the gaps are closed.
