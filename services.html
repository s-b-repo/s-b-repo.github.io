// service catalogue

Six focused services.

Every engagement follows the same principles: authorized in writing, scoped to your risk, delivered with evidence your team can act on. No scanner dumps. No checkbox audits. Just measurable security outcomes.

01

Custom Pentesting Tools

Bespoke offensive tooling built for your targets and rules of engagement. Recon frameworks, exploit chains, C2 and detection-bypass tooling, written in Rust, Go, C and Python.

  • Purpose-built for your stack
  • Memory-safe, maintainable code
  • You keep the source
02

Vulnerability Research & Exploit Development

Original security research that goes beyond scanning. We find the bugs others miss, then prove them with working exploits — zero-day discovery, reverse engineering, protocol analysis, and reproducible proof-of-concept development.

  • Zero-day discovery & responsible disclosure
  • Reverse engineering & protocol analysis
  • Reproducible PoCs & working exploits
03

Code Audits

Manual, security-focused source review — not just a linter. We hunt logic flaws, auth bypasses, injection, unsafe crypto and supply-chain risk.

  • Line-level vulnerability findings
  • Severity, impact & remediation
  • Re-test after fixes
04

Open-Source Security Audits

Deep audits of the open-source dependencies and tooling your business relies on. We read the code others assume is safe.

  • Dependency & supply-chain review
  • Reverse engineering & protocol analysis
  • Reproducible PoCs & upstream-ready fixes
05

Vendor Product Security Testing

Independent black-box and white-box assessment of third-party products and appliances before you trust them in production — so procurement decisions are based on evidence, not marketing.

  • Pre-purchase risk validation
  • Protocol & binary analysis
  • Clear go/no-go reporting
06

Detection Engineering & SOC Build-out

Project-based detection and response engineering — we design, build, tune, and hand over your monitoring stack, then it's yours to run. Wazuh/Elastic SIEM, log-ingestion pipelines, and DFIR automation fitted to your environment. Built and documented, not a managed subscription.

  • Wazuh/Elastic SIEM design & tuning
  • Log ingestion & normalisation pipelines
  • DFIR & alerting automation
  • Built, documented & handed over
